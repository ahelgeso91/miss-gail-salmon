A small boat, a short route home

Miss Gail is a Bristol Bay drift gillnetter. In 2026, our crew fished her waters and we're bringing part of that catch back to San Diego.

We keep this simple: fish caught by our crew, handled on the boat, shipped south, and sold directly. No long chain of middlemen between the boat and your kitchen.

Bristol Bay is a working fishery with strict limits and active in-season management. The fishery's future depends on the run, the rules, and people who follow both.

What you're buying

Wild sockeye from a managed fishery, handled by the people who caught it.

🐟

Wild fish

No pens or feed. Sockeye spend their lives in the ocean and return to Bristol Bay to spawn.

⚖️

Managed by the run

Alaska Department of Fish & Game monitors the runs and manages openings to protect enough fish for the next generation.
